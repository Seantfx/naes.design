Weekly overview, rethought

Lens Week

A week you can read at any zoom. Nothing is hidden, only compressed, until you look at it.

What the current view gets wrong

  • Seven equal columns on a 390px screen give each booking about 40px. Titles vanish, so color is the only information that survives.
  • Overlapping bookings shrink to slivers. Three parallel bookings and one solo booking look the same.
  • Revenue is one number floating above the grid, with no link to the days or bookings that earned it.
  • The only way to read a booking is to leave the week. Every check costs a round trip.
  • Nothing can be changed from the week. Moving a booking means opening it, editing a date field and coming back.

The idea

Keep the whole week on screen as a shape, always. Give the place you are looking at the room it needs to be read and acted on. Everything else compresses but never disappears, so you keep your bearings while you work.

Three moves

Focus day tap a column

The day you tap takes about half the screen. The other six compress into density strips that keep their color, order and overlaps. The day header shows that day's earned revenue and a load bar.

Time lens drag the lime band

Three hours on the rail get 2.5 times the height. Everything above and below stays visible at normal scale. With the focus day this becomes a two axis fisheye: wide where you look, dense everywhere else. Tap any hour label to jump the lens there.

Bundles three or more overlaps

Parallel bookings fold into one stacked card that shows the count, the time span and the revenue of the pile. Tap it and the pile fans out into a sheet where each booking is readable and actionable.

Revenue as a layer

Every number on the screen is money earned, not spent. Each booking card carries what it earns. Each bundle sums its pile. Each focused day header shows what the day earned, with pending underneath in amber. The week total at the top counts confirmed bookings only, with pending and cancelled money named separately, so the headline never promises revenue that has not happened yet. Confirm a pending booking and you watch it move from pending to earned across all four levels.

Direct manipulation

The week is not only a map, it is the workbench. Everything you would do in a booking form you can do on the grid.

Drag the grip
Every card in the focused day has a dotted grip on its right edge. Drag it to move the booking. A dashed slot follows your finger, snapped to 15 minutes, and turns amber if it would overlap another booking. Drop on a compressed strip to move it to another day. Mouse users can also long press anywhere on a card.
Drag the bottom edge
Changes the duration in 15 minute steps. The time label updates while you drag.
Tap an empty slot
Opens a new booking prefilled with that day and time. The plus button does the same for the focused day.
Reschedule
From a booking sheet, tap Reschedule, then tap the slot where it should go. Any day, any time.
Undo
Every move, resize, confirm and cancel shows a toast with an Undo button.

Supporting pieces

  • Status chips are legend and filter in one. Filtering dims instead of removing, so the shape of the week stays honest, and revenue recomputes for what you see.
  • Read mode turns the focused column into an agenda list while the six strips stay as the map.
  • Booking sheet shows client, notes, chair, duration and revenue, with Confirm, Message, Reschedule, Edit and Cancel.
  • Week navigation with the arrows at the bottom. Each week has its own bookings, and the month title follows.
  • Now line in lime with the current time, on the current day.
  • Compact header once you scroll the grid, so the timeline gets the room back.

How to use it

Tap a day
Focus it. Swipe left or right on the grid, or use the arrow keys, to move focus.
Drag the lime lens
Magnify a three hour window. Tap an hour label to jump.
Tap a booking
Open its sheet. Tap a bundle to fan it out.
Grid / Read
Switch the focused column between timeline and agenda.
Chips
Filter by status and watch revenue follow.

Not built yet, said plainly

  • Pinch on the rail to widen or narrow the lens from three hours to one or six. The lens is fixed at three hours.
  • Staff lanes inside the focused column, so a salon with four chairs reads as four sub columns instead of bundles.
  • Auto scroll while dragging near the top or bottom edge of the grid.
  • Persistence. Bookings you add or move live in the page and reset on reload.
  • Search and the people filter in the top bar are visual only.
